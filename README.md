# 🥛 Milk Dairy Pro

Welcome to **Milk Dairy Pro**! This is a complete, 100% offline, and installable Progressive Web App (PWA) designed specifically for dairy owners, milkmen, and farmers to manage their daily milk business with ease.

## 🚀 Key Features
* **Daily Milk Entry:** Quickly record morning and evening milk collection (Cow & Buffalo) with default or custom pricing.
* **Smart Ledger (Khata):** Automatically track customer dues (Baaki) and advance payments. 
* **PDF Billing & WhatsApp:** Generate professional monthly PDF bills and share them directly with customers via WhatsApp in one click.
* **Expense Tracker:** Add daily dairy expenses (cattle feed, salary, transport) to calculate your true Net Profit.
* **Visual Analytics:** View interactive charts tracking your day-by-day milk collection trends for any selected month.
* **100% Offline & PWA:** Install it directly on your phone's home screen. It works perfectly without an internet connection!
* **Secure Backup:** Easily export and import your entire data safely.

## 👨‍💻 About my app
Built to digitize and simplify dairy operations. This tool eliminates the need for paper diaries and manual calculations, helping dairy businesses run smartly and efficiently.
